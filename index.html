BUILD SOMETHING

Engineering Projects

Projects turn knowledge into actual engineering experience.

PROJECT 01

F1 Suspension

Design a simplified double-wishbone suspension. Create the control arms, upright, spring/damper and mounting points.

  • CAD assembly
  • Suspension geometry
  • Motion study
  • Design review
PROJECT 02

Mini Gearbox

Design a multi-stage gearbox with a target reduction ratio.

  • Gear ratios
  • Torque
  • Bearings
  • Shaft design
PROJECT 03

Aero Wing

Design a small adjustable aerodynamic wing and investigate how changing its geometry affects aerodynamic performance.

  • Airflow
  • Angle of attack
  • Downforce
  • Drag
PROJECT 04

Robotics Data Logger

Build a system that measures something on your robot and analyzes the data with Python.

  • Sensors
  • Data collection
  • Python
  • Graphs
PROJECT 05

Optimized Bracket

Design a lightweight bracket, identify its likely failure points and improve it.

  • Stress
  • FEA
  • Material selection
  • Weight reduction
PROJECT 06

Mechanical Teardown

Pick a mechanical device and reverse-engineer how every major component works together.

  • Reverse engineering
  • Measurements
  • CAD reconstruction
  • Design analysis
